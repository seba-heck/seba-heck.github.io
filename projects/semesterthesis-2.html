Abstract

This work presents a methodical approach to generate large-scale, high-resolution datasets of three-dimensional partial differential equations (PDEs), including Poisson, Advection-Diffusion, and Helmholtz equations. Using the FEniCS finite element framework, solutions are computed over a structured tetrahedral mesh with randomized, parameterized source terms and PDE coefficients. The method ensures physical diversity and numerical consistency while maintaining computational efficiency through controlled and tested sampling methods. The resulting datasets are designed to support data-driven modeling and machine learning applications in scientific computing, offering a foundation for training neural operators or foundation models in 3D PDE scenarios.
